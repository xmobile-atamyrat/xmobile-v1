# Chärjew glossary (extracted from src/i18n/ch.json)

Format: **ch form**, then standard tk in parentheses, then a ch.json example where useful.
Where ch.json uses two forms, both are listed, the more frequent one first.

## Catalog & shop

- **towar, towarlar** (haryt): Towaryň ady, Taza Towarlar, Towar barada (= specification)
- **produkt** (haryt): occasional, mainly "Taza produkt koş"
- **praýs** (baha): admin price records; "Praýs koş", "{count} praýs doredildi". Shopper-facing price stays **baha**.
- **praýs list** (baha sanawy)
- **skidka** (arzanladyş): Skidkadaki baha, Iň katta skidka birinji, Yanyp duran skidkalar (hot deals)
- **sklad** (ammar): Skladda bar, Skladda kalmady / Skladda ýok
- **nalicie** (elýeterlilik): availability filter
- **filtr, filtrler** (süzgüç)
- **sortirowka** (tertiple)
- **osnownoý** (esasy / deslapky): Osnownoý surat, Osnownoý kurs
- **glawny** (esasy / baş): Glawny surat, glawny stranisa
- **oçerd** (tertip): görkeziş oçerdi
- **görnüşläri** (görnüşler): variants/tags
- **abzor** (harydyň wideosy): product video/review
- **magazin** (dükan / üpjün ediji in procurement): Magaziniň ady
- **orginal / ofisialny garantiýa** (resmi kepillik)
- **lýubimiler** (halanlarym): Lýubimilere goş
- **logo** (logotip)

## Cart, order & delivery

- **korzina** (sebet): Korzina koş, korzinaga koşuldy, korzinadan aýryldy
- **zakaz, zakaz et** (sargyt, sargyt et): Zakazlarym, Hazyr zakaz et
- **pokupka** (söwda / satyn almak): Pokupkaňyzy dowam ediweriň
- **dostawka** (eltip bermek): Dostawka bahasy, Adresiňize dostawka
- **gelip almak** (özüňiz alyp gitmek): self-pickup
- **adres** (salgy)
- **naliçi / nagt** (nagt): naliçi töläň
- **töläg** (töleg), **oplata** (töleg in FAQ)
- **opşi** (jemi): total, subtotal, total amount
- **otmenit et, otmena, otmeneniýe sebäbi** (ýatyr, ýatyrylan, ýatyryş sebäbi)
- **status** (ýagdaý)
- **zakaz danny** (sargyt maglumatlary)
- **kaçandan / kaçanaça** (senesi from / to)
- **zwonok, zwonok et** (jaň, jaň et): Bahasy zwonokda (price on request)

## Account & auth

- **akkaunt** (hasap): Akkaunt aç (sign up), Akkaunda gir, Akkauntdan çyk, akkaundyňyz
- **parol** (gizlin söz): Parolyňyzy girkiziň, Paroly ýatdan çykardyňyzmy?
- **polzowatel** (ulanyjy): Polzowateliň ady
- **klient** (müşderi): Kliýent hyzmaty, Kliýent danny
- **nomer, telefon nomeri** (telefon belgi)
- **girkiz** (giriz): Adyňyzy girkiziň
- **munaka akkaunt** (such an account): Munaka akkaunt tapylmady
- **kette harp** (baş harp), **simwol** (nyşan)
- **podtwerždat** (tassykla): occasional; `tassykla` is also used
- **wostanawit et** (dikelt)
- **udalit et** (poz / öçür): accounts, rates

## Admin & system

- **admin, adminler** (dolandyryjy, işgär): Adminiň bellikläri
- **rol, roller** (wezipe)
- **stranisa** (sahypa): Stranisa tapylmady!
- **priloženiýa / prilojeniýe** (programma): Priloženiýa Dili, Mobilny priloženiýa
- **obnowleniýa, obnowit et** (täzelenme, täzele): app updates
- **ustanowit** (gurnamak)
- **nastroýka** (sazlamalar)
- **uwedomleniýa** (bildiriş)
- **soobşeniýe** (habar): chat messages, error message column
- **oşibkalar** (ýalňyşlyklar), **stroka** (setir)
- **istoriýa** (taryh)
- **danny, informasiýa** (maglumat)
- **lenta** (tasma): Işjeňlik lentasy
- **aktiw, aktiwny, aktiwnost** (işjeň): "banneriň aktiwnostyny aýyryň"
- **çistila / çistile** (arassala)
- **kopýala** (göçür)
- **period** (döwür), **data** (sene), **blok** (bölüm)
- **konwersiýa** (öwrülişik), **wyruçka** (girdeji), **srednii çek** (ortaça sargyt bahasy), **woronka** (basgançaklar), **seans** (sessiýa), **ustroýstwo** (enjam), **perehod** (geçiş), **gruppirle** (toparla), **poisk** (gözleg)
- **bazaga koş** (BASG goş)

## Native dialect words (not Russian)

- **ahtar** (gözle): Ahtar..., Polzowatel ahtar, Ahtaryş netijäläri
- **tez** (çalt), **çakgan** (çalt)
- **zor** (gowy / iň gowy): iň zor bahalar; **ýagşy** (gowy)
- **katta** (uly): Iň katta skidka
- **ýasa, ýasalan** (döret, döredilen)
- **haly** (entek): Haly surat ýok
- **owwa** (hawa), **koneçno** (hawa, in answers)
- **izä** (reddet)
- **salamaleýkum** (salam)
- **spasibo** (sag boluň)
- **boldy!** (üstünlik! / tamamlandy)
- **sän** (siz, chat label "You")
- **dep** (diýip)
- **kaýdyp gel** (dolan): Sebede kaýdyp gel
- **belle** (ýatda sakla, "save to profile")
- **barlap gör, barla** (synanyş)
- **gäräk** (gerek), **hökman ämäz** (hökman däl)
- **neçitä** (sany / quantity)
- **soň ýadyma sal** (soňra ýatlat)
- **bizär bilan** (biz bilen): rare, colloquial
- **ahtar / gözle** coexist; prefer **ahtar** for "search".
